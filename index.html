Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need Ozempic or Mounjaro to lose weight?

No. Most people lose weight by eating a little less than their body uses and moving more. That's what this plan helps you do, with normal Filipino food. If your doctor prescribed one of those medicines, keep following your doctor, and ask them before you change how you eat.

Do I need equipment?

No. A sturdy chair, a thick towel, a door, and a backpack you fill with water bottles or books. A 1-litre bottle weighs 1 kg.

Can I still eat rice?

Yes. You get a rice amount per meal for your size, from 1/2 cup to 1 1/2 cups. Every sample day has rice.

I've never worked out. Is it too hard?

No. Week 1 is sitting down to a chair and push-ups against a table. Everyone starts there. It grows with you.

I'm a woman. Is this for me?

Yes. Same plan. The food numbers have a women's version, and the sample days start at 1,500 calories. It won't make you bulky. Most people notice it first in how their clothes fit.

I have a lot of weight to lose.

This is a good place to start. No running, no jumping. If you have a health condition or take medicine, check with your doctor before changing how you eat.
